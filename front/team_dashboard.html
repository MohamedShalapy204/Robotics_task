Team Integration & Subsystems Architecture Dashboard

Subsystem Teams

Computer Vision Team

Responsible for giving the robot its "eyes" using Python and OpenCV.

Responsibilities

  • ✓ Camera setup and lens distortion calibration.
  • ✓ Color filtering (HSV masking) and size detection.
  • ✓ Mapping 2D pixel coordinates to 3D workspace coordinates.

Key Tasks

  • ➔ Write detector.py to stream the webcam feed.
  • ➔ Implement contour tracking and object bounding boxes.
  • ➔ Output a clean (X, Y, Color) data payload.

Integration Focus (CRITICAL)

To Kinematics: You must stream your 3D target coordinates to the Kinematics team. If your math is off, the arm will miss the object entirely.

With Structure: You rely heavily on the Structure team to mount the camera at a fixed, unmoving height so your coordinate conversion formula stays perfectly calibrated.

Kinematics & Simulation Team

The "Brain" of the operation. Translates vision data into physical arm movements using PyBullet.

Responsibilities

  • ✓ Inverse Kinematics (IK) calculations.
  • ✓ Path planning and collision avoidance.
  • ✓ Managing the PyBullet dry-run simulation environment.

Key Tasks

  • ➔ Load the robot's URDF model into PyBullet.
  • ➔ Write the pick-and-place looping logic.
  • ➔ Create the serial_handler.py stub to format commands.

Integration Focus (CRITICAL)

From Vision: You rely on Vision to provide accurate (X,Y) targets to feed into your IK solver.

To Embedded: Once the path is proven safe in simulation, you must convert the joint angles into Serial String Commands (e.g., M 120 45 90) and transmit them over USB to the Embedded team's Arduino.

Embedded Hardware Team

The muscle control. Responsible for the C++ code running directly on the Arduino microcontroller.

Responsibilities

  • ✓ Real-time motor control and PID tuning.
  • ✓ Parsing Serial commands from the Python host.
  • ✓ Implementing hardware interrupts for the Emergency Stop.

Key Tasks

  • ➔ Flash Arduino with firmware to control stepper/servo drivers.
  • ➔ Wire up limit switches for the Homing sequence.
  • ➔ Translate generic angles to specific motor step pulses.

Integration Focus (CRITICAL)

From Kinematics: You must strictly follow the Serial Protocol contract defined by the Kinematics team. If they send `M 90`, your parser must know that means Move 90 degrees.

With Power Supply: You rely heavily on the Power team to supply stable logic voltage (5V/3.3V) and motor voltage (12V/24V) without interference or brownouts.

Power Supply Team

The lifeblood of the system. Ensures stable, safe power distribution to all components.

Responsibilities

  • ✓ Voltage regulation and buck converters.
  • ✓ Total system amperage calculation and wire gauging.
  • ✓ Electrical safety and Emergency Stop relay wiring.

Key Tasks

  • ➔ Design a custom PCB or wiring harness for power distribution.
  • ➔ Wire the physical red Emergency Stop button to cut motor power.
  • ➔ Ground all components to a common ground to prevent signal noise.

Integration Focus (CRITICAL)

With Embedded: You must provide clean power. A sudden voltage drop when a motor starts moving will reset the Arduino, ruining the operation.

With Structure: You need to collaborate with the Structure team to ensure there is physical space and mounting points for heavy batteries and power supplies inside the base.

Structure & Frame Team

The physical foundation. Responsible for the mechanical integrity of the arm and workspace.

Responsibilities

  • ✓ Physical frame, motor mounts, and bearing alignment.
  • ✓ Camera stand and workspace table design.
  • ✓ Sorting bins and mechanical gripper design.

Key Tasks

  • ➔ CAD Modeling (SolidWorks/Fusion360).
  • ➔ 3D Printing, laser cutting, and physical assembly.
  • ➔ Generating the physical measurements (link lengths) for the software.

Integration Focus (CRITICAL)

To Kinematics: You must measure the exact distance between joints (in millimeters) and provide this data to the Kinematics team. If your CAD model differs from the printed part, their math will fail.

To Vision: The camera mount must be perfectly rigid. If the camera shakes or droops over time, the Vision team's calibration is ruined.
